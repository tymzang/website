RESEARCH PROJECT / 02 — COMPUTER VISION / IoT

NutriScale AI

A smart scale combining food images and measured weight to estimate nutritional information.

Computer visionHX711ESP32Offline AI
PROJECT / OVERVIEW

The challenge &
our approach.

Combines a load cell, HX711, camera, and AI to identify foods as ingredients are added step by step.

An intuitive nutrition-scale prototype with on-device processing.

01
Measure weight after each ingredient is added
02
Identify foods from camera images
03
Estimate nutrition from food type and measured weight

This page describes the research direction. It does not yet include live sensor data or device controls. An IoT dashboard may be integrated after authentication and data connectivity are in place.
